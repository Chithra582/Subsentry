# Recurring Spend Analyzer Skill

## Overview
Transforms fragmented billing schedules into unified monthly and annual expenditure analytics with category breakdowns.

## Operations
1. Converts weekly, quarterly, and annual fees into standard monthly equivalent values.
2. Computes total monthly burn rate and projected 12-month financial liability.
3. Categorizes expenses into Entertainment, Productivity, Infrastructure, Fitness, and Utilities.
4. Detects price hikes by comparing newly detected invoice values against historical baseline data.
