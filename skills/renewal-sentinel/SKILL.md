# Renewal Sentinel Skill

## Overview
Monitors upcoming renewal dates to ensure users are never surprised by unexpected auto-debits or trial conversions.

## Operations
1. Maintains an active calendar index of upcoming subscription billing dates.
2. Triggers proactive warnings at 72-hour and 24-hour intervals prior to billing events.
3. Highlights free trial expiration cliffs requiring urgent user cancellation decisions.
4. Dispatches alerts via in-app notification widgets, email reminders, or browser push.
